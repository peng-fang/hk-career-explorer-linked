# 香港就业市场探索器｜四章版现场演示

演示目标为 13 分 20 秒，余下 1 分 40 秒留给换页、切换和停顿。开场前将网页设为 English，使用新标签页从 00 开始。

| 时间 | 嘉宁的操作 | 英文口播 |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:30 | 在首页比较两地，指向较新行业截面后点 **Follow Jia Ning**。 | Jia Ning has just finished her master’s degree in Hong Kong. The two markets give her a starting point. Now she wants to see where she could use her analytical skills. |
| 1:30–5:45 | 看商业分析、SQL、可视化标签和任务规则。职业组预设为 **Professionals**。在热力表选择 **Financing and insurance** 的 919、**Information and communications** 的 925，分别点 **Add to comparison**。选 **Real estate** 的 26，再点 **Add as a benchmark**。进入 02。 | She wants to analyse business problems. The Explorer suggests occupation groups and keywords. Finance and information have nearly the same number of vacancies for professionals, but those vacancies make up 18.7% and 51.4% of each industry’s total. She keeps both options and adds real estate as a benchmark. |
| 5:45–8:15 | 指 20–24 岁与 25–29 岁的失业率。看高亮的收入大类。点 **Go to confirmation**，在核查栏记下 “What are the actual weekly hours?”，再点 **Confirm list and test my criteria**。 | The unemployment rates give her context for planning the search. The earnings chart highlights broader categories that cover her picks. She still wants to verify the job grade, actual hours and pay range for each opening. |
| 8:15–12:30 | 展示月薪、工时与预算门槛。把 **Weekly hours limit** 从 42 调到 44，点 **Apply criteria**，观察达标数由 2 变成 3。点 **Jump to shortlist**，打开地产行的 **Evidence and next checks**。 | Finance and information meet her three criteria at 42 hours. Real estate has a median of 43.2 hours in the pay survey’s industry segment. When she changes her limit to 44, the third pair qualifies. |
| 12:30–13:20 | 回到候选表，指招聘数据和薪酬统计的列名，收束嘉宁的行动。 | She will investigate finance and information first, using real estate as a comparison. Vacancy figures cover occupation groups. Pay and hours come from full-time employees in industry segments. She will check the details against real offers. |

若时间偏紧，可从 03 的 **Load Jia Ning’s example** 直接载入三个已确认的组合，集中展示 42 → 44 的结果变化。01 页可用行业下拉或多选按钮缩小热力表，投影较窄时比较两列会更清楚。
