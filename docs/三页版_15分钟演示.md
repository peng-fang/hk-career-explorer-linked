# 香港就业市场探索器｜三页版现场演示

用英文展示。以香港硕士毕业生嘉宁的三次决定为主线，演示约 13 分 30 秒，留 1 分 30 秒给停顿和操作。开场前用新标签页打开 00，切到 English。

| 时间 | 嘉宁在探索器里做什么 | 英文口播要点 |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | 介绍嘉宁和她的问题。 | Jia Ning has just finished a master’s degree in Hong Kong. She could apply in either market. She wants to know where to start, what jobs to look for, and whether an offer would cover the life she wants. |
| 1:00–3:00 | 在 00 快速指出年龄结构和青年就业背景，再看深港行业工资图，以金融举例。需要原币种金额时点开明细。 | Jia Ning looks at the workforce and youth unemployment for context. Finance pays above the local median in both Shenzhen and Hong Kong. The bars show where the industry sits in its own market. She decides to explore Hong Kong vacancies while keeping her Mainland search open. |
| 3:00–7:30 | 点 **Explore Hong Kong vacancies with Jia Ning**。先看背景和任务建议，再指气泡地图的空缺率与用工同比。在空缺热力表依次点金融及保险、资讯及通讯、地产的 **Professionals** 并加入比较。清单里为地产填实际工时的问题，再点 **Test pay, hours and living costs for these options**。 | Jia Ning wants to analyse business problems. The tool suggests tasks and occupation groups. The bubble chart puts vacancy intensity and employment change together. The heatmap shows the vacancies behind specific industry and occupation combinations. She saves finance, information and real estate so she can test them under the same personal criteria. |
| 7:30–12:00 | 在 02 对照月薪、每周工时和结余。点一个月薪中位数带入预算。把 **Weekly hours limit** 从 42 改到 44，点 **Apply criteria**。跳到候选表，展开地产行的 **Evidence and next checks**。 | At 42 hours, finance and information meet the three criteria using these industry benchmarks. The real-estate segment’s median is 43.2 hours. At a 44-hour limit, the third option qualifies. The candidate row keeps the question she wants to ask at interview. |
| 12:00–13:30 | 指候选表的招聘数据、工资与工时的口径，回到嘉宁的行动。 | She’ll investigate finance and information first and keep real estate on the list if the actual hours work for her. Vacancies come from occupation groups. Pay and hours refer to industry segments. When she gets an offer, she can replace the benchmark with the actual pay and check her monthly budget again. |
